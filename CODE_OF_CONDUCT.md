# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, welcoming and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the product.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report violations privately to **info@phineup.com** (Phine Up LLC). All reports are reviewed and treated confidentially.
